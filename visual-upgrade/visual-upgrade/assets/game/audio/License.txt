impact/ layered collision clips (22 files)
All eight source recordings below are marked Creative Commons 0 on their Freesound pages:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

"MTLimpt_Metal Impact Medium Bang Resonate x17" by harrisonlace (recorded on an empty car body)
https://freesound.org/people/harrisonlace/sounds/798843/
Used for transient and car-body light/heavy variants.

"Car Crash (with Glass)" by magnuswaker
https://freesound.org/people/magnuswaker/sounds/592388/
Used for short crash transient variants. Original sound was constructed from metal panels and glass.

"S36-09 Metal foley for car crash; much crunching.wav" by craigsmith
https://freesound.org/people/craigsmith/sounds/675475/
Used for metal deformation and light debris variants; vintage recording.

"Metal Scraping/Scratching" by JSilverSound
https://freesound.org/people/JSilverSound/sounds/615837/
Used as metallic scrape texture; source author describes processing of a field recording.

"metallic crash.wav" by BehanSean

Used for a short metallic transient.

"Glass Breaking" by AlterKartoffelsack
https://freesound.org/people/AlterKartoffelsack/sounds/465946/
Used for a quiet glass-detail tail.

"Hard Plastic Crack" by Doshke
https://freesound.org/people/Doshke/sounds/825839/
Used for a quiet plastic-detail tail.

"cinderblock scrape 6 and a thud.wav" by patchytherat
https://freesound.org/people/patchytherat/sounds/530989/
Used for the hard-surface scrape texture.

Changes to all impact sources: high-quality previews were selected as independent short excerpts,
downmixed to mono, filtered for their layer, cut near transient onset or crossfaded into loops,
and saved as 44.1 kHz 16-bit PCM with controlled peak headroom. Exact excerpt times,
source URLs/SHA-256 hashes and derived output hashes are in tools/prepare_impact_audio.py
and assets/game/audio/impact-bank.json. The previews are derivatives of the licensed
recordings, not the downloadable original full-resolution files.

The engine/road source URLs and SHA-256 checks remain in tools/prepare_audio.py.
