Club coupe visual asset

Original BMW 1 Series M model by Mike Pan, created for the Blender 2.7 demo.
Source: https://download.blender.org/demo/test/BMW27.blend.zip
Author: https://mikepan.com/bmw.html
Model license metadata: https://casual-effects.com/g3d/data10/index.html (BMW entry, Mike Pan and Morgan McGuire).
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC-BY-3.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

CoastalDrive changes: extracted one car from the original Blender scene; converted legacy Cycles materials to glTF PBR; removed badges and registration plate; normalized the wheel display pose, mirrored wheel orientation and winding; provided four independent wheel pivots and paint partitions; exported a self-contained Panda3D asset.
Editable source and scripts are in art/vehicles/club_coupe.blend and tools/blender/prepare_club_coupe.py.

The fictional Club C1 uses CoastalDrive generic simulation parameters, not BMW factory data. No endorsement by the artist or manufacturer is asserted.
