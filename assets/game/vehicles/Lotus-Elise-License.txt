CoastalDrive changes: extract the complete vehicle body, cabin, lamps, mirrors
and modeled wheels; omit the presentation environment, figure, baked animation,
removable soft top and antenna; adapt mesh proportions and rebuild normals for
the existing coastal-roadster hardware; convert materials to PBR and variable
paint; remove badges and blank the plate/hub textures; export four independent
wheel roots to BAM. No primitive body or wheel substitutes are used.
Thumbnail and contact shadow are rendered from this mesh using the project's
Poly Haven Skylit Garage CC0 HDRI (Greg Zaal).
