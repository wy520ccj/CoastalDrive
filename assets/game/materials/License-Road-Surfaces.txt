Original 2K JPG diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps retained verbatim;
leafy grass uses the original 2K diffuse. Source metadata, URLs, SHA256 and
fetch tool are archived in art/expressway/sources/road-materials/ and
tools/environment/fetch_photographed_surfaces.py.

Runtime adaptations: sRGB base-color sampling; linear data maps retaining
original channel count; 85% asphalt desaturation in the shader; 0.45 normal
XY strength; dry roughness minimum 0.55; approximately physical-scale UVs;
planar roadside texture mapping. No image pixels or physical road geometry
were changed. These textures affect appearance only, not grip or collision.
