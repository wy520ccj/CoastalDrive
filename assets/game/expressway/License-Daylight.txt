The original 4K HDR pixels are used unchanged for the sky. CoastalDrive aligns
the panorama around the vertical axis with its existing directional sun and
offline filters this same photograph to a 128px cubemap, spherical harmonics
and 64-sample roughness reflection mipmaps for daylight.env.
No runtime convolution and no generated painted sky substitute.
Source SHA256 and precise source URLs: daylight-manifest.json.
The source sun elevation is recorded in daylight-manifest.json; the retained
scene light is approximately 53.35 degrees. Yaw alignment keeps the horizon
horizontal; it does not change source sun elevation.
